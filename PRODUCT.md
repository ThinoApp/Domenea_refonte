# DOMENEA / TAO Passot - prototype context

## Product truth
DOMENEA presents TAO Passot, a premium residential real-estate project at Mont Passot in Nosy Be, Madagascar.

## Current prototype goal
Test a replacement visual language before the complete website redesign. The prototype is not the production site and does not attempt to carry all current sections or business logic.

## Confirmed content used here
- Location: Mont Passot, Nosy Be, Madagascar.
- Residential project with 2, 3 and 4 bedroom typologies.
- Existing DOMENEA materials describe approximately 20,000 sqm of land.
- The live site already uses architectural imagery, a hero video, a brochure conversion path and a VR experience.

## Direction commitments
- Architectural imagery and place lead the experience.
- Pinterest reference supplies the cinematic camera and architectural storytelling language.
- dsgn interior supplies the full-screen portfolio rhythm, peripheral interface and oversized spatial typography.
- DOMENEA / TAO content and identity remain the product truth. Competitor wording and imagery are not copied.

## Prototype constraints
- Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript.
- Temporary external imagery is explicitly non-production.
- Production typography, final imagery, full content architecture, SEO migration and business forms remain outside this prototype scope.
